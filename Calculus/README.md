# Calculus — Daily Learning

A personal learning archive documenting my journey through calculus.

The goal is not just to memorize formulas, but to understand the ideas behind
them through intuition, geometry, derivations, examples, and problem solving.

These notes are written during my learning process, so some explanations may
evolve or be corrected as my understanding improves.

---

##  Topics

Currently, this section covers:

- Limits and continuity
- Derivatives
- Geometric meaning of derivatives
- Rates of change
- Power rule
- Trigonometric derivatives
- Derivative formulas
- Functions and their behavior
- Mathematical intuition behind differentiation

More topics will be added as I progress.

---

##  Learning Approach

I use a combination of:

- Visual intuition
- Geometric interpretations
- Step-by-step derivations
- Worked examples
- Hand-drawn and self-created diagrams
- Mathematical notation
- Programming and experimentation where useful

The main objective is to understand:

> **What does this mathematical operation actually mean?**

rather than simply remembering a formula.
